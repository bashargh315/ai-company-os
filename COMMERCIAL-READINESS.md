# Commercial readiness checklist

- Confirm company/IP ownership
- Register/secure product trademark and domains as appropriate
- Finalize Terms of Service
- Finalize Privacy Policy and data-processing terms
- Audit open-source and third-party licenses
- Define AI-provider usage rights and customer-content ownership
- Add production authentication and account recovery
- Add database backups and disaster recovery
- Add security monitoring and audit logs
- Add billing provider after country/KYC eligibility review
- Add refund/cancellation policy
- Add customer support channel
- Run penetration/security review before launch
- Create separate staging and production environments
